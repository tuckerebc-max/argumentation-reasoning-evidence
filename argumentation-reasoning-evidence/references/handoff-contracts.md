# Argumentation handoffs

## From Close Reading — `HOF-CR-ARG-001`

Require located evidence, source claim, observed reasoning, alternative interpretations, source IDs, evidence IDs, and uncertainty IDs. Preserve source-analysis status.

## From QUEST — `HOF-QST-ARG-001`

Require claim candidates, evidence ledger, perspectives, method limitations, uncertainty, source IDs, evidence IDs, and provenance IDs. Treat missing support as a research lead when appropriate.

## From Writing — `HOF-WR-ARG-001`

Require task contract, claim, reasons, evidence, commentary, qualifiers, alternative, source IDs, evidence IDs, claim IDs, and learner decisions. Audit rather than replace the draft.

## To Writing — `HOF-ARG-WR-001`

Pass validated claim map, warrant audit, strongest alternative, selected response, source/provenance audit, confidence, uncertainty, and unresolved items. Writing must preserve scope and qualifiers.

## To QUEST — `HOF-ARG-QST-001`

Route evidence gaps, source conflict, causal gaps, method questions, or untested alternatives as explicit research leads.

## To PAR — `HOF-ARG-PAR-001`

Require competing claims, evidence ledger, strongest alternative, uncertainty, affected parties, authority, and decision rule before routing public disagreement.
